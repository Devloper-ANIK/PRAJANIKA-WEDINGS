Since 2016

The people behind the frames

"We started with one borrowed camera and a stubborn belief — that Indian weddings deserve films as cinematic as the love they celebrate."

Prajanika Studio began in a one-room Hyderabad office in 2016, shooting haldi ceremonies for friends who couldn't afford "real" photographers. Word travelled. Nine years later we're a twelve-person crew covering destination weddings across four states — and we still turn down work when we can't give it our full attention.

The name comes from the Telugu "prajna" — awareness, presence. Because that's the whole craft: being fully present for moments that will never happen again.

01

Craft over volume

We cap ourselves at 40 weddings a year. Every frame gets our full attention, or we don't take the booking.

02

Cinema, first

Stills that think like shots. Films cut like trailers. Every deliverable is directed, never merely "covered."

03

Built to outlive us

Archival prints, leather albums, 4K masters on redundant drives. Your grandchildren should hold this work one day.
